Research

What Annotation Quality Metrics Actually Certify

First author · with Rena Wei Gao (ByteDance LLM Foundation Team / University of Melbourne)

Under review · ARR
  • Asked whether inter-annotator agreement, classifier F1, LLM self-consistency and LLM replacement tests establish construct validity in multilingual NLP.
  • Evaluated 1,272 multilingual comments across seven theory-derived constructs, and identified three failure mechanisms: bundled definitions, extreme label sparsity, and collapse onto surface variables.
  • Benchmarked TF-IDF, XLM-R and mDeBERTa under grouped, leakage-safe evaluation, and validated internal metrics against an independent 550-participant survey.

Screen and Speech: Multimodal Fandom Discourse

Monash University · with Prof. Wong Wai Peng

Under review · ICWSM
  • Built a cross-platform corpus of 38,271 comments on seven girl groups from seven social media platforms, with linked media, platform and geographic information.
  • Designed a leakage-safe multimodal evaluation with video-disjoint splits, wrong-video controls, media-only probes, CLIP embeddings and mixed-effects models.
  • Found that aggregated media features gave no measurable predictive gain once video-identity leakage was controlled, while platform (venue) stayed significantly associated with cultural framing.

From Star IP to Cultural Export

PLS-SEM + construct-aligned multilingual comment analytics

GAAC 2026
  • Developed a mixed-method framework that combines PLS-SEM with multilingual social media comment analysis to study star IP, tourism promotion, M-Pop acceptance and cultural export.
  • Analysed 550 survey responses across eight latent constructs and tested 12 structural paths, with bootstrapping, MICOM and multi-group analysis.
  • Annotated 1,500 comments from 21 content groups; implemented TF-IDF + logistic regression, XLM-R with label-wise attention and asymmetric loss, and validation-tuned late fusion.
