About

I am a Master of Artificial Intelligence student at Monash University, and I will start an MS in Computer Science at the Georgia Institute of Technology in February 2027.

My research sits between natural language processing and computational social science. I am interested in three questions:

  • Do our evaluations measure what we think they measure? Construct validity of LLM annotation and evaluation, and when high agreement hides the wrong construct.
  • How do people talk about culture and media online? Leakage-safe multimodal and multilingual analysis of social media discourse.
  • How should agents decide under emotion and values? Emotion-aware narrative world models for long-horizon decision-making.

Before moving into AI, I earned a BA (Hons) in Marketing from Coventry University and studied financial law as an exchange student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University KoGuan School of Law. This background shapes my interest in the societal impact and governance of AI.

Research

2026 · Under review

What Annotation Quality Metrics Actually Certify

First author · with Rena Wei Gao (ByteDance LLM Foundation Team / University of Melbourne) · ACL Rolling Review

  • Asked whether inter-annotator agreement, classifier F1, LLM self-consistency and LLM replacement tests establish construct validity in multilingual NLP.
  • Evaluated 1,272 multilingual comments across seven theory-derived constructs, and identified three failure mechanisms: bundled definitions, extreme label sparsity, and collapse onto surface variables.
  • Benchmarked TF-IDF, XLM-R and mDeBERTa under grouped, leakage-safe evaluation, and validated internal metrics against an independent 550-participant survey.
2026 · Ongoing

Emotion-Aware Narrative World Model

Independent project · Detroit: Become Human

  • Building a narrative world model for long-horizon decision-making that tracks emotional state, relationships, morality, story progression and narrative arc.
  • Exploring multi-objective planning that balances successful outcomes with emotionally and narratively meaningful choices, rather than optimising for a single "best ending".
  • Designing evaluation for affect prediction, relationship dynamics, moral decisions and long-horizon planning.
2026 · Under review

Screen and Speech: Multimodal Fandom Discourse

Monash University · with Prof. Wong Wai Peng · ICWSM 2027

  • Built a cross-platform corpus of 38,271 comments on seven girl groups from seven social media platforms, with linked media, platform and geographic information.
  • Designed a leakage-safe multimodal evaluation with video-disjoint splits, wrong-video controls, media-only probes, CLIP embeddings and mixed-effects models.
  • Found that aggregated media features gave no measurable predictive gain once video-identity leakage was controlled, while platform (venue) stayed significantly associated with cultural framing.
2026 · GAAC 2026

From Star IP to Cultural Export

PLS-SEM + construct-aligned multilingual comment analytics

  • Developed a mixed-method framework that combines PLS-SEM with multilingual social media comment analysis to study star IP, tourism promotion, M-Pop acceptance and cultural export.
  • Analysed 550 survey responses across eight latent constructs and tested 12 structural paths, with bootstrapping, MICOM and multi-group analysis.
  • Annotated 1,500 comments from 21 content groups; implemented TF-IDF + logistic regression, XLM-R with label-wise attention and asymmetric loss, and validation-tuned late fusion.
