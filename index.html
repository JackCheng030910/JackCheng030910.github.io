Research & Publications

  • High Agreement, Wrong Construct: What Annotation Quality Metrics Actually Certify
    Xuejun Cheng, X. Xu, Rena Wei Gao
    Under reviewACL Rolling Review, Oct 2026
    Shows that inter-annotator agreement, classifier F1 and LLM consistency tests can all look strong while the model measures a different construct. Evaluated across 1,272 multilingual comments and seven theory-derived constructs, with an independent 550-participant survey as external evidence.
  • Screen and Speech: Aggregated Media Features Do Not Predict Fandom Discourse, but Venue Covaries with It
    Xuejun Cheng et al., with Prof. Wong Wai Peng
    Under reviewICWSM 2027
    A cross-platform corpus of 38,271 comments with leakage-safe multimodal evaluation (video-disjoint splits, wrong-video controls, CLIP, mixed-effects models). Media features add no predictive gain once leakage is controlled, while platform stays associated with cultural framing.
  • Emotion-Aware Narrative World Model
    Xuejun Cheng
    OngoingIndependent project · Detroit: Become Human
    A world model for long-horizon decision-making over emotional state, relationships, morality and story progression, with multi-objective planning that values emotionally and narratively meaningful choices rather than a single "best ending".
  • From Star IP to Cultural Export: Integrating PLS-SEM and Construct-Aligned Multilingual Comment Analytics
    Xuejun Cheng
    ConferenceGBA AI Alliance Conference (GAAC 2026) · DOI pending
    Combines a 550-response PLS-SEM survey with XLM-R-based multilingual comment analysis to study how star IP drives tourism promotion and cultural export.
